Autonomous PID Self-Balancing Robot

C++ / PlatformIO ESP32 Kalman Filtering PID Control
Key Constraints & Hardware:
  • ESP32 dual-core microcontroller, MPU6050 6-DOF IMU, DC gearbox motors with encoders.
  • Target control loop execution frequency: 100 Hz (10 ms period).
  • State Estimation: Implemented a discrete Kalman filter in C++ to fuse noisy accelerometer data with high-drift gyroscope rates, yielding a stable pitch estimate under dynamic vibration.
  • Control Architecture: Developed a cascaded PID loop (inner velocity loop, outer balance angle loop) to eliminate steady-state error and prevent integrator windup during motor saturation.
  • Failure Diagnosis & Fix: Identified high-frequency mechanical oscillation caused by sensor coupling to chassis flex; designed and 3D-printed a compliant TPU isolator mount to damp chassis vibration above 40 Hz.

Aerodynamic Design & Manufacture of Wind Turbine Blades

CATIA 3DEXPERIENCE Aerofoil Lofting Rapid Prototyping Wind Tunnel Testing
Key Constraints & Results:
  • Awarded 2nd place in engineering cohort testing out of all competing teams.
  • Manufacturing envelope: Laser-cut balsa laminates, carbon fiber load-bearing spar, tight mass limits.
  • Blade Geometry: Derived chord and twist distributions across spanwise sections using Blade Element Momentum (BEM) theory; generated continuous curvature surfaces via multi-section lofting in CATIA.
  • Manufacturing Strategy: Developed a staggered rib-and-spar interlocking internal structure cut from balsa and plywood, minimizing trailing-edge deflection under peak dynamic wind tunnel loading.
  • Validation: Evaluated physical prototypes in a low-speed wind tunnel against aerodynamic performance predictions, measuring power coefficient and stall onset angles.
